# Mentor questions

Questions for mentors, sponsors, and organizers. Ask each on the board with a title that starts `Mentor question:`, `Sponsor question:`, or `Organizer question:`, then list its id here. The board shows them in the order of the lines under "Questions to ask", each starting with its `[[q-xxxx]]` id; questions not listed come after.

Answers go on the board (`./hub answer`), with who said it, not on this page.
